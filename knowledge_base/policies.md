# Internal Support Policies

## Ticket Closure

A support ticket can be closed when the issue has been resolved and the customer has confirmed resolution.

If customer confirmation is not available, the support agent should not close the ticket based only on an assumption.

## Ticket Statuses

A ticket can have one of these statuses:

- OPEN
- IN_PROGRESS
- RESOLVED
- CLOSED

A ticket normally moves from RESOLVED to CLOSED after customer confirmation.

## Refund Policy

Support agents may initiate a refund request when the customer was charged incorrectly or when a service was not delivered.

Refunds above $500 require supervisor approval.

Support agents must not claim that a refund has been completed unless the payment system confirms the transaction.

## Customer Data

Support agents must not expose a customer's password, authentication token, or other secret credentials.

Only information necessary to resolve the support request should be disclosed.

## Escalation

A support issue should be escalated when:

- The issue involves a security incident.
- The requested action exceeds the agent's permissions.
- The available internal documentation does not provide enough information to safely answer the request.
